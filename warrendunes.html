⚠️ A quick heads up on alcohol

Warren Dunes enforces its no-alcohol policy strictly, so it's worth being careful here.

If anyone brings alcoholic beverages, keep them low-key:

  • Use containers that aren't obviously alcohol — no visible cans, bottles, or labels around the site.
  • Store them inside coolers tucked in the car, tent, or camper, out of plain view.
  • When in doubt, err on the side of being discreet.
