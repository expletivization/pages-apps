04

Dogs at the beach

Every crew's bringing a dog — here's where they can roam and swim.

🐾 Where they're welcome

Leashed dogs (6-foot max) are fine in the campground, on the trails, and on the boardwalks. Keep them off the main swim beach and the dunes.

🌊 Dog swimming

The dog beach runs north of the farthest beach parking lot, past the last swim buoy — about 2.5 miles of open shoreline where leashed dogs can wade and swim.

🥾 Getting there — not really a hike

It's roughly a 3-mile drive from the campground to the beach. Park at the far-north lot and walk north along the sand to the marked dog area. The steep ½–¾ mile dune climb with ~100 stairs is only the Weko Beach route, so skip that.

🎒 Bring along

Water and a bowl, plus booties or a plan for hot sand on paws. Never leave a dog in a parked car (park rule), and pack out all waste.

⚠️ A quick heads up on alcohol

Warren Dunes enforces its no-alcohol policy strictly, so it's worth being careful here.

If anyone brings alcoholic beverages, keep them low-key:

  • Use containers that aren't obviously alcohol — no visible cans, bottles, or labels around the site.
  • Store them inside coolers tucked in the car, tent, or camper, out of plain view.
  • When in doubt, err on the side of being discreet.
